# Licensing status

This is a development preview of the commercial DHMP Networking 1 product. The demo source, scene and hosting example are included in the package.

The repository's [licensing design](https://github.com/Perry3Dnl/DHMP/blob/main/docs/LICENSING_DESIGN.md) describes the intended model; it is not a final commercial license agreement. This preview does not introduce an additional license grant. Final distribution terms must accompany the commercial release.
