These network-path rates are measured from total packets completed across all active workers divided by the wall-clock duration of the whole Raw IPv6 or AF_XDP phase. They measure 1,408-byte-class Linux packet I/O and are deliberately not labeled as the normal DHMP processing ceiling. The AF_XDP speedup is only AF_XDP versus Raw IPv6 under this identical network workload.

Parallel without queue collisions

Every worker gets its own private veth pair and AF_XDP socket, preserving clear ownership while allowing the fast path to scale across CPU cores.

Big numbers are real aggregates

The headline packet, record and payload rates are combined wall-clock throughput from the server, not multiplication of a single-worker result.

Fair paired workload

Both implementations get the same worker count, packet count and payload. The run order alternates to reduce systematic first-run bias.
