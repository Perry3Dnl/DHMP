DHMP BEHAVIOR DEMO

Different jobs need different semantics.

DHMP keeps its data plane simple while allowing applications to choose how received records are published, how sends are completed, how rate limits behave, and whether an IoT device uses an established connection at all.

RATE POLICY

SmoothPacing

SPREAD LOAD

Records are spaced over time at the configured Pmax instead of intentionally releasing an entire one-second budget at once.

RATE POLICY

RejectWindow

HARD BUDGET

The fixed one-second Pmax budget is preserved. Sends beyond the current window are rejected instead of delayed into a smoother stream.
