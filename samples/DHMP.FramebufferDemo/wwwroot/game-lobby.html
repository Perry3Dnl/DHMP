Ingress scales linearly

Each connected player contributes one state update per tick. This part is generally modest even at tens of thousands of players.

Fan-out dominates

Outbound work is players × relevant peers × tick rate. Interest management is what makes very large lobbies physically possible.

Processing is not bandwidth

The live DHMP ceiling is an in-process logical processing result. The required wire rate shows the separate NIC/network problem.

This page is a capacity model, not proof that this many real Internet clients have been tested. It intentionally separates DHMP processing capacity from physical NIC, kernel, encryption, game simulation, connection-management and database limits.
